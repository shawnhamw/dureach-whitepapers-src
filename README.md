# Dureach Whitepapers

Research whitepapers from Dureach — the AI Revenue Workspace for B2B growth — on AI-driven go-to-market systems, published by Shawn Ham, Marketing Specialist at Dureach.

## Papers

### [AI-Driven Email, SMS, and WhatsApp Outreach: The Evolution of Marketing and CRM Architectures for SMBs](paper1.md)

How AI is reshaping outbound marketing and CRM for small and medium businesses: from AI-mediated revenue loops and signal-driven personalization to vendor landscapes, compliance and deliverability guardrails, and a pragmatic 12-week implementation roadmap.

### [Agentic Interoperability for Enterprise Marketing: MCP, Agent-to-Agent Systems, and the Emerging Multi-Agent Stack](paper2.md)

A technical guide to the emerging multi-agent stack for enterprise marketing: the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol, provider comparisons, security and governance, and the economics of agentic systems.
